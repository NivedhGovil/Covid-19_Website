Prevention and Safety Measures

There are many ways to protect yourself and others from COVID-19.

Basic Safety Steps

  • Wash your hands often with soap and water for at least 20 seconds.
  • Use hand sanitizer if soap and water are not available.
  • Wear a mask when around other people, especially indoors.
  • Keep a safe distance (about 6 feet) from others.
  • Stay home if you feel sick or have symptoms.

Extra Protection

  • Get vaccinated to lower the risk of serious illness.
  • Cover your mouth and nose when you cough or sneeze.
  • Avoid crowded places and poorly ventilated rooms.
  • Clean and disinfect surfaces you touch often, like doorknobs and phones.

Healthy Habits

Eating healthy food, getting enough sleep, and exercising can help your body stay strong.
