Global Impact

COVID-19 affected almost every part of daily life across the world. It was not just a health crisis, but also a social and economic one.

Schools and Education

Many schools closed and students had to learn online. Teachers and families worked hard to keep education going.

Jobs and Work

Lots of people lost jobs or had to work from home. Businesses changed how they operated to stay open.

Travel and Movement

Airports, trains, and buses were shut down or limited. People could not travel freely between countries for a long time.

Health Systems

Hospitals became very busy and doctors had to care for many patients at once. This showed how important strong health systems are.

Community and Society

People learned to help each other more. Communities supported the sick, shared food, and found new ways to stay connected online.

Global Cooperation

Countries worked together to share information, create vaccines, and find solutions to fight the pandemic.
