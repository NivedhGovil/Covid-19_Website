Introduction to COVID-19

COVID-19 is a disease caused by a virus called SARS-CoV-2. It first appeared in late 2019 and spread quickly around the world.

The virus mainly spreads when people breathe in tiny droplets from someone who is infected. It can also spread by touching surfaces and then touching your face.

Most people who get COVID-19 have mild symptoms like fever, cough, and tiredness. But some people, especially older adults or those with health problems, can become very sick.

COVID-19 led to a global pandemic. This means it affected almost every country, changing daily life, schools, jobs, and travel.

Scientists and doctors worked hard to understand the virus, create vaccines, and find ways to protect people.
