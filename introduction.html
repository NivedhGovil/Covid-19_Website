Introduction to COVID-19

COVID-19 is a disease caused by a virus called SARS-CoV-2. It first appeared in late 2019 and spread quickly around the world.

The virus mainly spreads when people breathe in tiny droplets from someone who is infected. It can also spread by touching surfaces and then your face.

COVID-19 led to a global pandemic, meaning it affected almost every country, changing daily life, schools, jobs, and travel.

Scientists and doctors worked hard to understand the virus, create vaccines, and find ways to protect people.
