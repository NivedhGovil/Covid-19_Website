COVID-19 Variants

Variants are new versions of the coronavirus. Viruses change over time, and sometimes these changes make them spread faster or act differently.

Why Variants Matter

  • Some variants spread more easily between people.
  • They can cause different symptoms or make illness more severe.
  • Scientists study variants to see if vaccines and treatments still work well.

Examples of Variants

Over time, several major variants appeared:

  • Alpha: First found in the UK, spread quickly in 2020.
  • Delta: First found in India, caused strong waves of infection in 2021.
  • Omicron: First found in South Africa, spread very fast worldwide in late 2021.

What Scientists Do

Scientists keep track of variants by testing samples from patients. This helps them understand how the virus is changing and how to protect people better.
