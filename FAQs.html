Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is COVID-19?
 A: It is a disease caused by a virus called coronavirus (SARS-CoV-2).

Q: How can I stay safe?
 A: Wash hands, wear a mask, keep distance, and avoid crowded places.

Q: Why are vaccines important?
 A: They help protect people from serious illness and reduce the spread of the virus.

Q: What is "long COVID"?
 A: Some people continue to feel tired, have trouble breathing, or face other health problems for weeks or months after infection.

Q: Can children get COVID-19?
 A: Yes, children can get infected, but most have mild symptoms. Vaccines are available for many age groups.

Q: Can pets get COVID-19?
 A: Some pets, like cats and dogs, can catch the virus, but it is not common. They usually have mild illness.

Q: Do masks really help?
 A: Yes, masks reduce the chance of spreading the virus, especially indoors or in crowded places.

Q: What should I do if I test positive?
 A: Stay home, rest, drink fluids, and avoid contact with others. Seek medical help if symptoms get worse.
