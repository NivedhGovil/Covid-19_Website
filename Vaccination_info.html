Vaccination Information

Vaccines help protect people from getting very sick with COVID-19. They train the body to fight the virus if it enters.

Why Vaccines Are Important

  • They lower the chance of severe illness and hospitalization.
  • They help reduce the spread of the virus in communities.
  • They protect vulnerable groups like older adults and people with health problems.

Types of Vaccines

Different vaccines were developed around the world. Some use mRNA technology, while others use viral vectors or protein-based methods. All aim to help the immune system recognize the virus.

Booster Shots

Boosters are extra doses given after the first shots. They help keep protection strong, especially when new variants appear.

Global Vaccination Programs

Many countries started large vaccination campaigns to protect their people. These programs helped reduce deaths and allowed schools, jobs, and travel to reopen more safely.
