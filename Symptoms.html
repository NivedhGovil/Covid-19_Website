Symptoms and Diagnosis

COVID-19 can cause many symptoms. They usually show up 2 to 14 days after infection.

Common Symptoms

  • Fever or chills
  • Cough
  • Tiredness
  • Loss of taste or smell
  • Sore throat or runny nose
  • Muscle aches and headache
  • Nausea, vomiting, or diarrhea

Serious Symptoms

If someone has trouble breathing, chest pain, confusion, or lips turning blue, they should get emergency help right away.

Diagnosis

Doctors use tests like PCR or rapid antigen tests to find out if someone has COVID-19.
